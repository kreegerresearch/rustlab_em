# rustlab_em — Rendered Curriculum

A self-study course in classical electromagnetics, built around
[rustlab](https://github.com/kreegerresearch/rustlab) simulation scripts paired
with theory notes. Each lesson is a single executable Markdown notebook —
derivations, code, and plots in one file.

What you see here is the **rendered** output of the source notebooks: prose,
executed `rustlab` blocks, captured `print()` output, and inline SVG plots.
GitHub displays it directly.

The source notebooks live at [`../notebooks/<slug>.md`](../notebooks/).
This `book/` tree is regenerated by `make notebooks` from the repo root;
only `book/README.md` (this file) is hand-written.

## Reading order

Each lesson depends on the ones before it.

### Foundations

| # | Lesson | Focus |
|---|--------|-------|
| 01 | [Vector Calculus & Fields](01-vector-calculus-and-fields.md) | grad/div/curl on grids, line & surface integrals, the divergence and Stokes' theorems numerically |
| 02 | [Electrostatics & Coulomb's Law](02-electrostatics-coulomb.md) | point charges, dipoles, rings; field lines and superposition |
| 03 | [Gauss's Law & Electric Potential](03-gauss-law-and-potential.md) | flux, $\nabla\cdot\vec{E}=\rho/\varepsilon_0$, potential $V$, equipotentials |

### Materials, geometry & static BVPs

| # | Lesson | Focus |
|---|--------|-------|
| 04 | [Geometry & Material Maps](04-geometry-and-material-maps.md) | rasterizing shapes into spatial $\varepsilon(x,y)$, $\mu(x,y)$, $\sigma(x,y)$ arrays |
| 05 | [Poisson & Laplace BVP — Dielectrics & Conductors](05-poisson-laplace-bvp.md) | $\nabla\!\cdot\!(\varepsilon\nabla V) = -\rho$ via finite differences and sparse solves |
| 06 | [Magnetostatics & Vector Potential](06-magnetostatics.md) | Biot-Savart, Ampère, $\nabla^2\vec A = -\mu_0\vec J$ with material $\mu_r$ regions |
| 07 | [Faraday's Law & Induction](07-faraday-induction.md) | induced EMF, self- and mutual inductance, eddy currents |

### The full Maxwell set

| # | Lesson | Focus |
|---|--------|-------|
| 08 | [Maxwell's Equations](08-maxwell-equations.md) | displacement current, Poynting vector, charge & energy conservation |
| 09 | [EM Waves](09-em-waves.md) | wave equation from Maxwell, plane waves, polarization, standing waves |

### Numerical EM solvers

| # | Lesson | Focus |
|---|--------|-------|
| 10 | [FDFD — Frequency-Domain Maxwell Solver](10-fdfd-frequency-domain.md) | complex-valued sparse curl-curl solve on a Yee grid; transmission and scattering |
| 11 | [FDTD — Time-Domain, Dispersive Materials, PML](11-fdtd-simulation.md) | Yee grid, leapfrog, TF/SF source, Drude/Debye/Lorentz via ADE, split-field PML |

### Devices

| # | Lesson | Focus |
|---|--------|-------|
| 12 | [Waveguides, Cavity Eigenmodes & Radiation](12-waveguides-and-radiation.md) | generalized eigenvalue problem for waveguide & cavity modes; near-field to far-field transform |
| 13 | [Transmission Lines, S-Parameters & Antennas](13-transmission-lines-and-antennas.md) | $Z_0$ from geometry, telegrapher + VSWR, multiport S-parameters, 73 Ω half-wave dipole |
| 14 | [Device Walkthrough — 2-D Microstrip Cross-Section](14-capstone-device-simulation.md) | layered cross-section → material map → 2-D FDTD → driven probe spectrum (no patch mode, no $S_{11}$, no 3-D gain pattern) |
| 15 | [Lumped Capacitance Extraction](15-lumped-capacitance.md) | energy and Gauss $C$, capacitance matrices, MIM and bond-pad parasitics, virtual-work force |
| 16 | [Smith Chart & Impedance Matching](16-smith-chart.md) | bilinear $\Gamma$ map, L-match / stub / $\lambda/4$, synthetic patch locus |
| 17 | [Lumped Inductance Extraction](17-lumped-inductance.md) | loop and solenoid $L$, mutual matrix, Nagaoka coefficient, plunger force |

## Local interactive view

For Plotly hover/zoom/pan and KaTeX-rendered math, run `make html` from
the repo root and open `index.html` (right next to this file) in a
browser. The HTML build includes an auto-generated entry page with
prev/next navigation between notebooks. The `index.html` and
per-notebook `*.html` files are gitignored — regenerate them locally
any time.

## How this is organized

The pattern (sources under `notebooks/`, rendered output committed to a
top-level `book/`) is documented at
[`docs/lesson-site-pattern.md`](../docs/lesson-site-pattern.md) so other
rustlab-based course projects can adopt it.
